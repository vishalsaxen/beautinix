Version 1.0, 25 October 2026. Salon owners must agree to these terms, and to the platform fee of ₹200 per month, before they can sign in or create an account in Salon de Beauté. The text shown in the app lives in `index.html` (`TERMS`); keep both in step.

## 1. About Beautinix

Beautinix is an online platform that lists beauty salons and lets guests find them and book appointments. These terms apply between Beautinix and the salon (“you”) that uses Salon de Beauté.

## 2. Your account

Your account is created with the unique code the Beautinix team sends you. Keep your password private. You are responsible for everything done with your account, and you must tell us at once if you think someone else is using it.

## 3. Platform fee: ₹200 per month

You agree to pay Beautinix a platform fee of ₹200 (two hundred rupees) per month, plus any applicable taxes. The fee starts from the date your account is created, is payable in advance for each month and is not refunded for part of a month. If the fee is unpaid for more than 15 days after it is due, Beautinix may hide your salon from guests until it is paid. We will tell you at least 30 days before any change to the fee.

## 4. Your salon details

Your salon name, contact details, services, prices and availability must be accurate and kept up to date. Prices are in Indian rupees and are what the guest pays at your salon. You must hold any licences your services need.

## 5. Bookings and payments from guests

A booking is an agreement between you and the guest. Guests pay you directly at the salon; Beautinix does not collect or hold that money. Honour bookings you accept, and if you can't, contact the guest on WhatsApp as early as you can.

## 6. Guest information

Use guests' names, phone numbers and emails only to handle their bookings. Don't share them, sell them or send unrelated marketing.

## 7. Fair use

Don't post false, offensive or misleading content, copy other salons' listings, or try to interfere with the platform or other users' accounts.

## 8. Ending your account

You can stop using Beautinix at any time by telling the Beautinix team; the fee stops from the next month. Beautinix may suspend or remove a salon that breaks these terms or doesn't pay the fee.

## 9. Liability

Beautinix provides the platform as it is and is not responsible for the services a salon provides to guests. To the extent the law allows, Beautinix's total liability to you is limited to the platform fees you paid in the last 3 months.

## 10. Changes and law

We may update these terms and will tell you in the app before changes take effect; if you keep using Beautinix after that, you accept the new terms. These terms are governed by the laws of India.
